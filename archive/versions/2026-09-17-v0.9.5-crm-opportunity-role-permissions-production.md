## Delivered / 已交付

- Added organization-scoped `crm.opportunity.read`, `crm.opportunity.create`, and `crm.opportunity.update` to the existing fail-closed central permission service. Company owner, organization administrator, manager, and member roles receive read/create/update; read-only receives read; platform-admin status alone grants no tenant opportunity access. / 在现有默认拒绝中央权限服务中增加组织范围的商机读、建、改；企业所有者、组织管理员、经理和成员可读建改，只读角色仅可读，纯平台管理员身份不能取得租户商机权限。
- Protected direct opportunity list/detail/create/update routes and the opportunity collection embedded in customer detail. Denials and organization reads persist bounded permission evidence; audit failure returns `503` before body parsing. / 保护商机列表、详情、创建、更新及客户详情内嵌商机；拒绝和组织读取写入有界证据，审计失败在解析请求体前返回 503。
- Matched the pre-parser permission guard to the complete Express/Phase4 route surface, including case variants, trailing slashes, invalid IDs, and noncanonical IDs. Only canonical safe integers enter `target_id`; other path segments are represented as `null` rather than recorded. / 早期权限守卫覆盖 Express/Phase4 的完整路由形态，包括大小写、尾斜杠、非法及非规范 ID；只有规范安全整数写入目标 ID，其余以 null 表示，不记录原始路径片段。
- Preserved the current CRM pages while adding semantic View/Edit buttons, permission-gated editing, a read-only opportunity modal, dialog focus management, server-text escaping, and explicit non-2xx handling. / 保留当前 CRM 页面，新增语义化查看/编辑按钮、权限控制编辑、只读详情态、对话框焦点管理、服务端文本转义及明确的非成功响应处理。
- Contacts, tasks, hard delete, ownership transfer, entitlements, quotas, CRM layout, M3/M4, AI/knowledge, influencer, Feishu, workflow, exports, and frozen PPT behavior remain outside this release. / 联系人、任务、硬删除、所有权转移、权益、配额及其他业务模块和冻结 PPT 均未扩改。
