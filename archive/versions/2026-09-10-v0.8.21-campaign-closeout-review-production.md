## Delivered / 已交付

- The existing M4 Campaign workspace now exposes one `结案复盘` command without adding a page or replacing the approved shell. It is enabled only for an active, writable, `settled` Campaign; a `reviewed` Campaign is projected as complete. / 既有 M4 Campaign 工作台新增一个“结案复盘”操作，不新增页面或替换已批准壳层；仅活动、可写且处于 `settled` 的 Campaign 可执行，`reviewed` Campaign 显示已完成。
- The accessible review dialog captures title, executive summary, outcomes, reusable methods, problems/root causes, next actions, an optional customer-report reference, and private/team visibility. / 可访问复盘弹窗采集标题、管理摘要、项目成果、可复用方法、问题与根因、下一步行动、可选客户报告引用及个人/团队可见性。
- A new permission-scoped server snapshot reads every non-cancelled Campaign collaboration independently of current UI filters or pagination, verifies completed/settled evidence and currency, and aggregates safe creator-payment/client-receipt totals without copying sensitive payment references. It rejects more than 5,000 collaborations explicitly. / 新增受权限约束的服务端快照，不受当前界面筛选或分页影响地读取全部未取消 Campaign 合作，校验完成/结算凭证和币种，并汇总安全的达人付款/客户回款金额而不复制敏感支付参考号；超过 5,000 条时明确拒绝。
- Human confirmation archives one `campaign_review` under `tm-business-artifact-v1`, creates an exact `knowledge` and `review` link pair, and then advances the returned Campaign version from `settled` to `reviewed`. Stable idempotency and stale/lost-response recovery prevent duplicate knowledge. / 人工确认会按 `tm-business-artifact-v1` 归档一条 `campaign_review`，建立精确的 `knowledge` 与 `review` 双关联，再使用返回版本将 Campaign 从 `settled` 推进到 `reviewed`；稳定幂等和陈旧/丢失响应恢复可避免重复知识。
- Existing influencer import, field filters, saved views, column workspace, collaboration custody, Feishu, AI/knowledge, and frozen proposal-PPT behavior is unchanged. / 既有网红导入、字段筛选、保存视图、列工作区、合作保管、飞书、AI/知识及冻结方案 PPT 行为保持不变。
