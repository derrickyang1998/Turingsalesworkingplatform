## Delivered / 已交付

- Customer detail now returns at most 100 same-organization tasks, including owner display name, status, due time, description, completion evidence, and `meta.tasks`; open tasks are ordered first by due time, followed by recently updated closed tasks. / 客户详情现在最多返回 100 条同组织任务，包含负责人、状态、截止时间、说明、完成证据和任务元数据；待处理任务按截止时间优先，已关闭任务按更新时间排列。
- The detail route requires `crm.task.read` in addition to the existing customer, opportunity, and contact permissions before dispatch. Existing organization/team/customer scope remains authoritative. / 详情接口在原有客户、商机和联系人权限之外增加任务读取权限，且继续以组织、团队和客户范围为权威边界。

- Task creation defaults to the current customer owner and team. Successful mutations refresh the same detail view with stale-response protection and focus restoration. / 新建任务默认使用当前客户负责人和团队，写入成功后刷新同一客户明细，并保留陈旧响应保护和焦点恢复。
