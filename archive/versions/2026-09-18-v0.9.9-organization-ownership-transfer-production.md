## Delivered / 已交付

- Organization ownership now transfers through an atomic compare-and-swap service. The request must name the current organization, expected authority version, target active member, bounded reason, and exact target username. / 组织所有权现通过原子 compare-and-swap 服务转移，请求必须携带当前组织、预期权威版本、有效目标成员、有界原因及精确目标账号确认。
- Both the previous and new owner sessions are revoked in the same transaction. The previous owner remains an organization member, and the authoritative owner row increments its version with updater and timestamp metadata. / 原所有者与新所有者的全部会话在同一事务中撤销；原所有者保留组织成员身份，权威所有者记录递增版本并记录更新人及时间。
- Every successful transfer appends an immutable audit event containing bounded before/after facts; secrets, cookies, bearer tokens, and plaintext credentials are excluded. / 每次成功转移追加不可变审计事件，仅保存有界前后状态，不记录密钥、Cookie、令牌或明文凭据。
- The existing Admin Organizations and Members screen adds a transfer modal with organization/current/new-owner context, explicit risk warning, reason validation, exact-username confirmation, and disabled-by-default submit. No page replacement or early interface was introduced. / 现有管理控制室“组织与成员”页面增加转移弹窗，展示组织、当前/新所有者、风险提示、原因校验和账号精确确认，提交默认禁用；没有替换页面或回退界面。
- M3/M4, AI/knowledge, influencer, Feishu, workflow, exports, customer dual pages, and frozen PPT remain unchanged. / M3/M4、AI/知识库、网红、飞书、工作流、导出、客户双页面及冻结 PPT 均未改动。
