## Delivered / 已交付

- The approved 20-column Chinese upload template and historical 19-column aliases remain supported. CSV, JSON, and XLSX files now follow one guided flow: upload preview, field mapping, row validation, and hash-bound confirmation. / 保留已批准的 20 列中文上传模板及历史 19 列别名；CSV、JSON、XLSX 统一执行上传预览、字段映射、逐行校验和文件哈希绑定确认。
- Preview returns a versioned mapping contract, file SHA-256, total/valid/error/blank counts, bounded samples, and proposed aliases without writing to influencer or knowledge tables. Confirm rejects a changed file or mapping. / 预览返回版本化映射合同、文件 SHA-256、总行数及有效/错误/空白计数、有界样例和建议别名，且不写入网红或知识表；确认时拒绝已变化的文件或映射。
