- The Admin control room can set a canonical UTC expiry or restore a perpetual term without replacing history. The accepted CRM, influencer, AI/knowledge, proposal/report, and PPT experiences are unchanged. / 管理控制室可设置规范 UTC 到期时间或恢复永久期限，不覆盖历史；既有业务界面未回退。
