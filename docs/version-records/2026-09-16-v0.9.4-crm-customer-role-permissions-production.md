## Delivered / 已交付

- Extended the central module/action service with organization-scoped `crm.customer.read`, `crm.customer.create`, and `crm.customer.update`, derived only from live user, organization membership, member policy, company-owner authority, and team membership facts. / 将中央权限服务扩展到 CRM 客户读、建、改，权限仅从实时用户、组织成员、成员策略、企业所有者和团队成员事实推导。
- Applied read gates to customer list, detail, statistics, dashboard, public pool, and compatible dashboard aliases; applied create/update gates to lead conversion, customer creation/update, claim/release/assign, and archive-result operations. / 客户列表、详情、统计、看板、公海和兼容看板接口接入读取权限；线索转客户、创建、更新、认领/退回/分配及结果归档接入创建或更新权限。
- Enforced exact tenant scopes: company owner and organization administrator can use organization scope, manager can use team scope, and member is limited to personal and public-pool scope. A platform role without an active tenant role receives no CRM customer grant. / 精确执行组织、团队、个人与公海范围；纯平台角色不能代替有效租户角色获得 CRM 客户权限。
- Projected approved actions through login and `/api/auth/me`; the accepted customer dashboard/detail UI remains unchanged structurally and hides or blocks existing write and broad-scope controls when the server denies them. / 登录态向现有界面投影服务端允许操作，客户看板与客户明细双页面结构不变，无权控件被隐藏或阻止。
- Persisted bounded permission evidence for denials and organization-wide reads without recording search text or credentials. Opportunities, contacts, and task module permissions remain outside this release. / 对拒绝和组织级读取写入有界审计，不记录搜索文本或凭据；商机、联系人和任务模块权限不在本版范围。
